# RedRelay — 90-Second Hackathon Presentation Script

> **Presenters**: Lead Architect / AI Systems Engineer  
> **Target Audience**: Hackathon Judges, Clinical Directors & Technology Leaders  
> **Time Limit**: 90 Seconds  

---

### [00:00 – 00:15] — The Hook & The Unspoken Medical Crisis
*(Presenter clicks "Launch 90-Second Guided Rescue Demo" or opens Landing Page)*

> "Judges, imagine a mother in severe obstetric hemorrhage at Victoria Hospital. Her surgeon urgently needs two units of O-negative Packed Red Blood Cells.
> 
> Today, locating blood online is easy—finding blood that **actually exists, is physically in the fridge, and can survive cold-chain transport** is a nightmare. Families and doctors spend 90 desperate minutes calling disconnected phone numbers only to arrive at empty shelves or spoiled units."

---

### [00:15 – 00:35] — Innovation A & B: Proof of Availability & Mission Control
*(Presenter clicks "Next Step" to advance to Step 3 / Mission Control Radar)*

> "This is **RedRelay — The AI Emergency Blood Rescue Network**.
> 
> Notice our first innovation: **Proof of Availability**. Unlike directories that treat stale database listings as real blood, RedRelay rejects unverified listings and isolates three strict tiers: **REPORTED**, **CONFIRMED**, and **HELD**.
> 
> Our deterministic coordination engine screens candidate banks in Bengaluru, filters exact immunohematology compatibility, and locks an active 20-minute reservation with Rotary TTK Blood Centre."

---

### [00:35 – 00:65] — Innovation C: The Signature Wow Moment (Failure-Aware Rescue Rerouting)
*(Presenter clicks the glowing red "Simulate Disruption" button!)*

> "Now, here is the differentiator that sets RedRelay apart from any chatbot or directory: **Failure-Aware Rescue Rerouting**.
> 
> Let's simulate a real-world emergency disruption: Rotary TTK experiences a local trauma surge and must withdraw their reserved units before release.
> 
> Watch the screen. **RedRelay NEVER silently substitutes blood.** 
> 
> Our AI Sentinel immediately flags the disruption, preserves the evidence in our ledger, and instantly ranks NIMHANS Blood Centre as the pre-verified backup. 
> 
> Crucially, our **Human Approval Gate** requires Dr. Rao to formally authorize the reroute. One click, and the mission diverts responsibly without breaking clinical protocol."

---

### [00:65 – 00:80] — Innovation D: Proof of Handoff & IoT Telemetry
*(Presenter clicks Step 9 & 10, showing the Courier Telemetry & Final Handoff)*

> "During transit, our mobile-first courier console streams real-time IoT temperature telemetry—holding steady at 3.8°C within the strict 2-to-6°C DGHS standard.
> 
> At destination, our **Proof of Handoff** uses short-lived cryptographic QR tokens to confirm delivery. No patient data is exposed in the QR codes, and every transition is immutably sealed with SHA-256 hash chaining."

---

### [00:80 – 00:90] — Innovation E & The Closing Vision
*(Presenter shows the Audit Ledger & AI Readiness Dashboard)*

> "Existing apps help you find blood numbers. RedRelay demonstrates how AI-assisted coordination ensures **verified availability, handles operational disruptions, and proves the handoff**.
> 
> All 13 core invariants are validated with automated tests.
> 
> **RedRelay: Every unit. Every minute. Every handoff.** Thank you!"
